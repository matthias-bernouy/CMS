Initial 1.0.0 release: publish Stripe Connect payment and seller contracts with integration-owned Connection settings, credential validation, idempotent owned webhook reconciliation, saved and applied revisions, and structured health. Provider credentials remain scoped secret references. Installation succeeds before provider configuration; local provider simulation remains explicit.

Signing secrets are bound to the Stripe account, destination, and endpoint through hashed provisioning receipts. Reconciliation preserves valid bindings across API-key rotation and rejects unverified legacy or mismatched secrets. A failed vault write cannot confirm the new revision or report readiness; recovery requires restoring the matching signing secrets. Health checks inspect remote configuration and stored provisioning receipts without mutating Stripe resources.
