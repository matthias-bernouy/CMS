Preparing auth screens

Published on June 24, 2026 - 4 min read

Auth screens can exist before their endpoints are connected, as long as they stay honest about their state.

Separate interface from wiring

The starter includes login, registration, password recovery and verification screens as static editable pages.

Connect behavior later

When the auth provider is ready, these pages can bind inputs and submit actions to the public auth API.

The UI should prepare the workflow without pretending the integration is already active.
